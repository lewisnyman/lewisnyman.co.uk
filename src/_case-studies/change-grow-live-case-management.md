## The challenge

Change Grow Live's 17-year-old case management tool was cumbersome and restrictive, turning client interaction into a data gathering exercise instead of encouraging strong relationships.

## My role

I worked alongside a product team and leadership to redefine the tool's focus. We reintroduced paper conversational guides and I rapidly prototyped new concepts for better person-centered care.

## Impact

- The CEO hailed it as "The most exciting thing I've seen at this organisation in a long time"
- Overwhelmingly positive staff feedback, with over 90% preferring the new interface
