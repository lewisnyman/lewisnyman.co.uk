## The challenge

The primary challenge was to understand the specific needs and challenges of senior public sector leaders and translate those into a central digital service that would effectively facilitate connection, collaboration, and experience-sharing across different government bodies.

## My role

I led the end-to-end design process, which included:

- Planning and executing user research with senior leaders
- Synthesising and presenting research findings to senior Cabinet Office staff
- Providing advice on next steps for the service
- Ensuring the project met the GDS Service Standard at each stage
- Delivering ongoing user research during the alpha and beta phases to iteratively improve the platform concept

## Impact

The project successfully delivered a platform that allowed senior public sector leaders to connect, collaborate, and share experiences. My work ensured that the service was built on a strong foundation of user needs research and met government standards, culminating in the planning and delivery of a valuable digital service for public sector leadership development.
