## The challenge

Change Grow Live, the UK's largest national drugs and alcohol support charity (50+ locations), had numerous, inconsistent, and difficult self-referral forms, frustrating potential clients.

## My role

I collaborated with the product team to radically simplify the self-referral form by reducing, rephrasing, and clarifying questions. I re-designed the layout, controls, and buttons for clarity and usability.

I created prototypes for testing, learning, and iteration, providing evidence for wider design improvements across the organisation.

## Impact

- 80% reduction in completion time
- 34% more successful referrals
